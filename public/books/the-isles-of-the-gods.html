Featured October 5, 2026

The Isles of the Gods

by Amie Kaufman

Selly wants to reach her father. A prince's secret voyage pulls her into a conflict far beyond her own plans.

On our shelves: Fantasy · Young Adult

WHY WE PICKED IT

Before the gods stir, a young woman wants her life back

Selly's first concerns are wonderfully concrete. She has errands to finish, a captain whose demands frustrate her and a father she is determined to reach. In the published opening of The Isles of the Gods, she is also buying magical supplies while hiding the magician's marks on her own hands. The world is strange; her impatience with being kept from the life she wants is easy to recognize.

Amie Kaufman lets the larger trouble enter through the ordinary business of a port. Prices are changing, supplies are scarce and people ask sailors whether war is coming. Meanwhile, a royal flotilla is decorated for festivities. Selly notices the distance between those two experiences. That gives her irritation with privilege some substance before the prince's voyage draws her into the danger herself.

We picked this novel because it offers a personal way into a large fantasy conflict. Selly already has a purpose of her own when Prince Leander's need to cross the sea takes over her plans. Readers who enjoy a reluctant alliance can begin with that friction: whose journey matters, who gets to decide, and what happens when neither person can afford to walk away?

The story combines a sea voyage, magic, romance and the threat of waking gods. It is the first half of a duology, followed by The Heart of the World, so readers have a clear next book if they want to continue. We recommend it particularly to young adult fantasy readers who like a sharp-voiced heroine and an adventure that grows out of competing loyalties. Selly's own unfinished business is a good reason to step aboard.

THIS ONE IS FOR

Young adult fantasy readers who enjoy seafaring adventures, mythology, political danger and romance. Start with this book before continuing to The Heart of the World.

MEET THE FEATURED AUTHOR

Amie Kaufman

Amie Kaufman is a New York Times and internationally bestselling author of fantasy and science fiction. Her books include The Isles of the Gods, the Elementals trilogy and collaborations such as the Illuminae Files and Aurora Cycle. Her work has been translated into nearly thirty languages. She also hosts the writing podcasts Amie Kaufman on Writing and Pub Dates.
