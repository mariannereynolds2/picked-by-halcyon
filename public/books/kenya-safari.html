Kenya Safari: A Guide for the First-Time Visitor

by Jim Walker

The kind of safari guide that answers the questions you only realize you have once the trip starts feeling real.

Buy on Amazon US (opens in a new tab)Read why we picked it

Why we picked it

Why we picked it

What I like about Kenya Safari is that it does not approach a first safari as though excitement is the only thing a traveler feels. There is excitement, of course, but there is also the practical uncertainty: what do you pack, what should you leave at home, what will the days actually feel like, and which small details can make the difference between feeling prepared and feeling overwhelmed?

Jim Walker writes from the perspective of someone who went, paid attention and came back wanting the next traveler to feel more ready. The book blends practical guidance with the rhythm of his own January 2025 safari, so the advice is attached to real situations rather than floating as generic travel tips. His long background in travel and wildlife photography also gives the wildlife side of the book a natural visual sensibility.

I’d recommend this before the booking confirmation turns into a countdown. It works for someone planning Kenya for the first time, but it also has enough travelogue in it for the person who is still only dreaming about the trip. It is useful without feeling clinical, and enthusiastic without pretending that good preparation is optional.
