The Secretary

by Britney King

Gillian's dream job offers a dangerous bargain: how much can an employer ask once saying no no longer feels safe?

On our shelves: Mystery & Thriller

WHY WE PICKED IT

When an opportunity becomes a debt

The unsettling part of The Secretary's premise is how ordinary the first invitation looks. Gillian Martin needs her job at Shergar Corp. Her employer pays well, offers attractive benefits and shows a personal interest in her. An invitation to dinner arrives as a simple yes-or-no choice. Then another note asks her to help conceal a murder.

That change is our reason for selecting the book. A choice made freely at the beginning can become the means by which someone claims a much larger obligation later. King's setup gives thriller readers a concrete question to argue over: at what point does gratitude for an opportunity turn into a dangerous willingness to protect the person who provided it?

Gillian's need for the job matters to that conversation. It complicates an easy judgment from outside the situation. A reading group can examine ambition and coercion together, asking what information she has, what she stands to lose and whether a powerful employer has made refusal a realistic option. The appeal lies in following those pressures into a deliberately heightened workplace nightmare.

Expect adult psychological suspense involving murder, manipulation and an unequal relationship with an employer. This is the opening volume of a two-book Secretary story in King's After Hours catalogue. We recommend it for readers interested in compromised choices and dark workplace power. Readers wanting a gentle office romance or a self-contained account of the entire duet should choose with those distinctions in mind.

THIS ONE IS FOR

Adult thriller readers and discussion groups interested in workplace power, ambition and coercion. The first volume of the Secretary duet; the employer-employee attraction is part of a dangerous suspense premise.

Editorial selection based on the author's published premise, biography and description of the Secretary duet. This is a recommendation note, not a full-book review.

MEET THE FEATURED AUTHOR

Britney King

Britney King is a Texas-based author who has published psychological thrillers since 2013. Her catalogue includes the Water, New Hope, Killer and After Hours series as well as standalone books. Her fiction frequently explores obsession, control and morally complicated choices behind apparently comfortable lives.
