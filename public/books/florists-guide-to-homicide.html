TODAY'S PICK · COZY MYSTERY

A Florist’s Guide to Homicide

by Tara Lush

Flowers, Florida, a fresh start and the deeply inconvenient problem of finding your rival dead on the beach.

Pre-order on Amazon ↗Visit Tara Lush ↗

WHY WE PICKED IT

The reason this one made the desk.

This has the exact kind of setup that makes a cozy mystery easy to settle into. Clara Whitman leaves a failing marriage and Boston winters behind for Sunny Shores, Florida, where she is trying to rebuild her life around a pink 1972 flower truck. It is cheerful, specific and just a little eccentric, which means the murder lands exactly where a cozy reader wants it to: right in the middle of a world that already feels fun to spend time in.

The dead woman is Sunny Shores’ other florist, Sherri Sanford, and Clara has already had a very public clash with her. Suddenly a business rivalry looks like motive. What keeps the book from feeling like a puzzle sitting by itself is the company Clara has around her: Paisley, her boundary-free sixty-something roommate, Herbie the Brussels Griffon, Detective Gabriel Cypress, a Garden Club full of secrets and a whole town that seems to be growing more than flowers.

This is one for readers who want a mystery without having to live in darkness for three hundred pages. Tara Lush leans into humor, found family, midlife reinvention, a little slow-burn romantic energy and zero-gore coziness. It is the kind of book you pick up for the murder and keep reading because you like the people and want another afternoon in their town.

THIS ONE IS FOR

For cozy mystery readers who like quirky small towns, midlife heroines, found family, pets, light romance, humor and low-gore sleuthing.
