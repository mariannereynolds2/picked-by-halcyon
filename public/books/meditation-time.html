The story

Meditation Time: Know Yourself and the World Around You

Born from the crucible of profound personal adversity, Meditation Time: Know Yourself and the World Around You is a deeply moving philosophical exploration of existence, resilience, and the art of living. At age seventeen, a catastrophic diving accident left Canadian author Laurent Grenier quadriplegic, stripping away the physical freedom of his youth and confronting him with life’s most urgent existential question: Why live?

Over four decades of rigorous contemplation, Grenier transformed that question into a guide for navigating the human condition. Rather than offering quick fixes or rigid self-help formulas, this collection of concise, interconnected essays invites readers to slow down, reflect, and examine reality with clarity and honesty. Drawing from philosophy, science, and lived experience, Grenier bridges the inner landscape of self-awareness with the interconnectedness of the external world. He challenges conventional illusions of control, showing instead how wisdom, agency, and contentment emerge when we align our thoughts with reality rather than fighting against it.

Designed to be savored and studied rather than rushed, Meditation Time serves as both a mirror and a compass. Grenier’s journey from despair to hard-won enlightenment stands as living proof that even when external circumstances narrow our physical boundaries, the mind remains vast. Ultimately, this book invites readers to awaken to the present, deepen self-understanding, and discover enduring meaning in simply being alive.

Why readers may enjoy it

Readers will love this book because it replaces empty self-help clichés with hard-won philosophical wisdom forged through four decades of living with quadriplegia. Its short, interconnected essays offer reflections on acceptance, resilience, and human agency. By blending philosophy, science, and mindfulness, Laurent Grenier provides a refreshing antidote to modern hustle culture, showing how peace can come from aligning with reality rather than trying to control it.

Who it suits

For thoughtful readers seeking grounded, practical philosophy—especially those navigating major life transitions, adversity, or burnout. It suits readers who appreciate concise, reflective essays and want deeper self-awareness and resilience without religious dogma.

About the author

Laurent Grenier

Laurent Grenier is a philosopher born in France in 1957 to French Canadian parents and raised in Canada. In 1974, as he had grown into an athletic teenager, he suffered a physically disabling neck injury in a diving accident that shattered his dreams of an athletic career. Thereafter, Laurent Grenier dedicated himself to decades of meditation and study, in an effort to develop an art of living and a vision of things that make the world understandable and habitable.
