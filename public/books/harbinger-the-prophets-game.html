Harbinger: The Prophet's Game

by Steven Harrison

A killer leaves a black feather beside each victim, drawing Detective Sam Rourke into a network of corruption that protects Chicago’s elite.

Buy on Amazon UK (opens in a new tab)Read about the book

The story

Three victims. Three methods. One black feather.

Chicago’s wealthiest citizens are being murdered and displayed for the city to see. Beside each victim is a black feather. The press calls the killer the Harbinger, a self-appointed judge targeting people whose money and influence have kept them beyond consequence.

Detective Sam Rourke has no interest in the mythology. Military-trained and carrying the weight of an old war, a fading faith and a father disappearing into dementia, Rourke believes the evidence will tell him what happened if he is patient enough to follow it.

When the killer contacts disgraced investigative reporter Miles Carter, Miles sees a chance to reclaim the career he lost. Rourke sees someone trying to get ahead of the investigation by deciding which version of the story the public hears first.

With each murder, the evidence leads deeper into powerful law firms, fraudulent property developments and Chicago politics. The victims begin to look like pieces of something built to stay buried. And the closer Rourke comes to understanding why they were chosen, the harder it becomes to separate justice from vengeance.

Harbinger: The Prophet’s Game is the first book in the Sam Rourke Thriller Series.

About the author

Steven Harrison

Steven Harrison is a British crime thriller author and the creator of the Sam Rourke Thriller Series. He began his working life building PCs and spent his career in IT, solving problems and teaching himself whatever the next shift in technology demanded. That habit of following clues and questioning easy answers now shapes his fiction.

His debut novel, Harbinger: The Prophet’s Game , introduces a military-trained detective whose pursuit of a serial killer exposes the people and institutions that protect Chicago’s powerful. Steven publishes independently through his imprint, Steven Harrison Creative.
