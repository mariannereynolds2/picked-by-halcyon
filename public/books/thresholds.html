TODAY'S PICK · SPIRITUAL MEMOIR

Featured October 3, 2026

Thresholds

by Gerry van den Brekel

Diary fragments trace a personal search through loss, identity and spiritual experience.

Amazon Kindle ↗Amazon paperback ↗

On our shelves: Memoir & Biography · Faith & Spirituality · Nonfiction

WHY WE PICKED IT

A life examined through its crossing places.

Gerry van den Brekel introduces Thresholds as a collection of diary fragments and questions about how she became the person she is. That framing matters. A diary is written before a life has arranged itself into a convincing story; it can hold uncertainty that a polished account of transformation might hurry past.

The published description places pain, loss and rebirth alongside an exploration of unseen dimensions. Readers' Favorite describes twenty reflective pieces, each followed by questions. We picked the book for that invitation to pause. A question at the end of a personal account asks the reader to do something different from simply agreeing with the person telling it.

The spiritual framework is specific to van den Brekel's perspective. Her account includes experiences she understands in supernatural and esoteric terms, informed by a background in traditional healing and movement practices. Readers can approach those as her interpretations while considering the familiar human concerns beneath them: grief, belonging and the effort to understand what has happened to us.

This is the pick for someone drawn to spiritual life writing who is comfortable sitting with an open question. A useful way into it would be to read a piece, then write down what its closing questions bring up in your own life. The diary format and reflective prompts are the reasons to consider it for that slower kind of reading.

THIS ONE IS FOR

Readers interested in spiritual memoir, reflective journaling and personal accounts of loss and identity, with an openness to the author's esoteric perspective.

MEET THE FEATURED AUTHOR

Gerry van den Brekel

Gerry van den Brekel is a writer and practitioner whose work explores embodiment and consciousness. Her official biography describes a background in the corporate world, traditional medicine, and teaching Chinese and Tibetan martial arts. Thresholds draws on personal diary fragments.
