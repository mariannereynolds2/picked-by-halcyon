TODAY'S PICK · VAMPIRE ROMANTASY

Blood & Stone in Scotland

by Jade Christy

A Scottish castle, a man who should not still be there, and a romance that keeps getting harder to walk away from.

Buy on Amazon ↗Visit Jade Christy ↗See it on Goodreads ↗

WHY WE PICKED IT

The reason this one made the desk.

There’s a very particular reading mood this book hits: you want atmosphere first, danger close behind it, and romance simmering underneath everything. Blood & Stone in Scotland drops an independent property flipper into an ancient Scottish castle she thinks she bought outright, only to find Lord Tristan Sinclair still living there and insisting the sale never happened. From there, the story keeps tightening the space between irritation, curiosity and attraction.

What makes the setup fun is that the castle is not just scenery. Locked rooms, villagers who go quiet, old books, hidden histories and Tristan’s suspiciously intimate knowledge of the past all turn the setting into part of the mystery. The romance grows inside that tension, so the supernatural reveal matters because the relationship has already given you a reason to care.

This is an easy recommendation for readers who like vampire romance with actual atmosphere around it. If forced proximity, Scottish castles, slow-burn chemistry, hidden supernatural worlds and a protective immortal hero sound like your kind of weekend, this one knows exactly what experience it is promising. It is also the first book in a complete four-book series, which makes it especially tempting if you prefer discovering a story you can keep reading instead of waiting months for the next installment.

THIS ONE IS FOR

For readers who like vampire romantasy, gothic atmosphere, forced proximity, Scottish settings, slow-burn tension and bingeable series.
