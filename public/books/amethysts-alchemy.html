TODAY'S PICK · ADULT ROMANTIC FANTASY

Featured October 9, 2026

Amethysts & Alchemy

by Rachel Rener

A pharmacist who draws magic from minerals must reconsider the rival she thought she understood.

Amazon ↗Author website ↗Reader reviews on Goodreads ↗

On our shelves: Fantasy · Romance

WHY WE PICKED IT

A gift that changes the explanation

Delaney Stone has built a working life around an ability she once had no explanation for: consuming minerals allows her to draw out their magic. As a pharmacist, she conceals that gift within the remedies she makes. Amethysts & Alchemy caught our attention because its fantasy begins with someone whose competence and unusual needs occupy the same life.

Her rivalry with rock-shop owner Heath Spencer gives the adventure its friction. An opportunity to visit an ancient copper mine in China places them together, and the journey challenges what Delaney believes about him. The published setup promises mineral magic, travel and a rivals-to-lovers relationship, with an emotional-support pig adding a markedly playful note.

For a reading group, the strongest opening question is about interpretation. What changes when a character discovers that an experience once explained as a problem has another meaning? Delaney's ability belongs to a fictional magic system, but the broader conversation about being understood can still matter to readers. Her changing view of Heath offers a companion question: how much of a rivalry rests on evidence, and how much on a story we keep telling ourselves?

Rener presents this as cozy romantic fantasy with neurodivergent representation. Cozy here describes the intended tone; it does not mean the romance is closed-door. Adult sexual content and the book's irreverent humour make it a different choice from an all-ages fantasy adventure. Start here for the Amethysts & Alchemy series.

THIS ONE IS FOR

Adult romantasy readers who enjoy a distinctive magic system, rivals-to-lovers banter and neurodivergent characters. Best for groups comfortable discussing adult romance alongside identity and assumptions.

Editorial selection based on the author's book and series information, her published romance-content guidance and reader responses. This is a recommendation note, not a full-book review.

MEET THE FEATURED AUTHOR

Rachel Rener

Rachel Rener writes romantic fantasy through Lightning Conjurer Books. Her series include Gilded Blood, The Lightning Conjurer, The Bone Whisperer and Amethysts & Alchemy. Her author website describes her interest in flawed heroines, unusual magic systems and romance, and hosts her Rachel's Renergades reader community.
