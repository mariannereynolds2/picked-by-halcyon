The Spy Keeper of Marseille

by Roseanna M. White

A widowed mother leads a Resistance network while learning whether she can trust someone with her own heart.

Amazon (opens in a new tab)Read why we picked it

Why we picked it

Protecting a family can demand an impossible choice

Zelie Bellerose wants her children to have a future in a free France. That wish takes her into the Resistance and eventually into the leadership of an intelligence network. It is a difficult premise for a mother: the work she undertakes for her children's sake also places their safety, and her presence in their lives, under threat.

That tension is why we selected The Spy Keeper of Marseille. Courage here has a domestic cost. Roseanna M. White's discussion guide asks readers to consider Zelie's decision to send her children away so that she can continue the work. It is a question worth sitting with before reaching for an easy judgment about what a good parent ought to do.

The romance brings another kind of trust into that situation. Marcel Laurent, a concert pianist and former prisoner of war, conducts a youth orchestra while gathering intelligence; his broadcasts also carry coded messages. Music gives the two a shared language, even as their work requires concealment. For readers drawn to love stories under pressure, the attraction is in that difficulty of opening yourself to someone when secrecy has become part of survival.

White drew on the life of Resistance leader Marie-Madeleine Fourcade, fictionalizing and compressing events for the novel. We recommend approaching it as Christian historical romance with an espionage story and a family at its heart. Published in July 2026, it stands among White's historical romances while sharing a world with The Collector of Burned Books. That companion title offers another way into her wartime fiction after this feature.

About the author

Roseanna M. White

Roseanna M. White is a bestselling, Christy Award-winning novelist whose work includes historical romance, biblical fiction, mysteries and fantasy romance. Her series include Shadows Over England, The Codebreakers and The Imposters. In The Spy Keeper of Marseille, she draws on the history of France's Resistance to tell a fictional story of leadership, motherhood and love.
