The Cold Shore

by Rachel Amphlett

An abandoned boat, a brutal discovery and a detective facing the responsibility of his first homicide case.

On our shelves: Mystery & Thriller

WHY WE PICKED IT

The case arrives before confidence does

The opening of The Cold Shore begins with curiosity. Alan Wendle spots a battered sailboat stranded on the north Kent coast and goes to investigate. Once he is aboard, the rising tide makes even an ordinary step uncertain. Then he finds the body. That physical instability gives the discovery its force: danger is already under his feet before he understands what he has walked into.

Gavin Piper arrives with the strain of an earlier investigation still visible on him. He intended to assist his superior, but the case becomes his to lead. There is something compelling about that particular kind of pressure. People need decisions from him now; becoming comfortable with the responsibility will have to wait.

We chose this book for readers who enjoy the work of an investigation alongside the person doing it. The murder gives Gavin a problem to solve, while the unfamiliar coastal community and his inexperience complicate how he approaches it. The premise promises escalating danger when another witness is killed. Our interest is in what happens to a detective's judgment when the cost of hesitation keeps growing.

This is the opening book in Gavin Piper's series, a spin-off from Rachel Amphlett's Kay Hunter novels, and its October 5, 2026 publication makes it a timely point of discovery. Start here if you want to meet a detective at the beginning of his own series. Readers drawn further into Amphlett's crime fiction can also explore Scared to Death, the first Kay Hunter novel.

THIS ONE IS FOR

Readers of British crime thrillers who enjoy coastal settings, police investigations and a detective with something to prove. The opening extract contains a graphic description of the murder victim.

MEET THE FEATURED AUTHOR

Rachel Amphlett

Rachel Amphlett is a USA Today bestselling crime author whose catalog includes the Detective Kay Hunter and Detective Mark Turpin novels, Dan Taylor espionage thrillers and short crime fiction. Her books are available in print, ebook and audio. Before writing fiction, she worked in publishing and radio and played guitar in bands. The Cold Shore introduces Detective Gavin Piper's own series.
