ARCHIVED PICK · POLITICAL TECHNO-THRILLER

Silent Code

by Jessie Clark

A cyberattack inside the White House turns every trusted system into a possible weapon.

Buy from the publisher ↗Find on Amazon ↗Visit Jessie Clark ↗

WHY WE PICKED IT

The reason this one made the desk.

Silent Code starts with a premise that feels frighteningly close to the world we already live in. Secret Service agent Emily Jensen is trained to notice danger before it reaches the President, but a cyberattack inside the White House exposes something much worse than an outside breach: a conspiracy hiding inside the systems she is supposed to trust.

The appeal here is not simply hacking jargon or another chase through government corridors. The real tension comes from uncertainty. If the infrastructure itself has been compromised, who can Emily believe? Every discovery widens the threat, and the book keeps its pressure on loyalty, institutional power and what happens when truth becomes dangerous enough that powerful people would rather bury it.

This is the thriller I’d hand to someone who likes contemporary conspiracies with momentum. It feels built for readers who enjoy capable protagonists, political pressure, hidden networks and technology being used as leverage rather than decoration. It is Book One of the Silent Code series, so there is room for the larger world to keep expanding once the first case gets its hooks into you.

THIS ONE IS FOR

For readers of cyber thrillers, political suspense, government conspiracies, smart heroines and fast-moving modern danger.
