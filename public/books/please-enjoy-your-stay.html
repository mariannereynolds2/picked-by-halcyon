Please Enjoy Your Stay

by Tara Goedjen

A true-crime podcast revives a murder Mia thought was solved, sending her back to an Austrian castle that may have hidden the real killer.

On our shelves: Mystery & Thriller

WHY WE PICKED IT

The witness who returns to the scene of her certainty

When Mia was young, a murder at a castle in the Austrian Alps changed the course of her life. Her testimony helped put a man behind bars after her cousin's death, but the case also made her family a subject of public suspicion. Ten years later, a true-crime podcast resurrects the story, and the convicted man is appealing. Mia has spent years living with the consequences of a certainty that now seems less secure.

To protect her aunt and discover what was overlooked, Mia returns to the castle, now being renovated as a luxury hotel. Working as a nanny gives her a reason to stay close to the scene without immediately revealing her purpose. Then somebody disappears and the investigation begins to raise the possibility that the wrong person was convicted.

Please Enjoy Your Stay draws tension from two forms of storytelling. Mia has the memory of what she witnessed, while the podcast is building another version for a mass audience. What happens when strangers reshape a tragedy into entertainment? How should Mia respond if the story that protected her family may also have hidden the truth? These are unusually rich questions for a thriller discussion.

We chose Goedjen's adult debut because it combines the atmosphere of gothic suspense with contemporary anxieties about media, memory and justice. The secluded setting, powerful family secrets and escalating danger should satisfy mystery readers. More importantly for book clubs, Goedjen publicly offers a discussion guide and welcomes enquiries about potential video or in-person visits.

THIS ONE IS FOR

Adult mystery and thriller clubs interested in gothic settings, false certainty, family secrets and the ethics of true crime. Includes murder, disturbing discoveries and psychological tension.

BOOK CLUB CONVERSATION

If questioning your memory might overturn a conviction, do you pursue the truth even when it threatens the people you love?

MEET THE FEATURED AUTHOR

Tara Goedjen

Tara Goedjen writes atmospheric mysteries and thrillers. Her earlier books include the young-adult mysteries The Breathless and No Beauties or Monsters; Please Enjoy Your Stay is her adult-fiction debut. She has an MFA and a PhD, has worked in academia, and previously edited fiction for a Sydney-based publisher. Her years living in different places shape her interest in memorable settings. She also provides reading-group materials on her official website.
