TODAY'S PICK · PHILOSOPHICAL SPECULATIVE FICTION

Featured October 4, 2026

Flat Earth Machine

More Than Just a Novel

by Gary J. Head

What can one person’s memory preserve inside a world that controls its own explanation?

Amazon Australia ↗Explore the author’s book site ↗

On our shelves: Horror & Science Fiction

WHY WE PICKED IT

Start with the person doing the witnessing.

The Witness is the most useful doorway into Gary J. Head’s fictional world. In the author’s character material, this figure has no command over the Machine. What matters is the ability to stay present and remember an experience that cannot subsequently be rewritten. That gives an enormous speculative system a recognisable human concern: keeping hold of what happened to you.

There is a difference between possessing information and being able to live with what you have seen. The Witness material makes room for fear, grief and love in that process. Our interest lies there, in the possibility that a person’s emotional life might matter to the machinery of a fictional universe. A reader can disagree with an explanation and still be interested in the experience that produced it.

Head’s own introduction describes the novel as an invitation to explore through the Witness rather than arrive at a prescribed belief. His engineering background helps explain why the accompanying website keeps returning to how a system’s parts relate. Readers who enjoy examining a fictional world after closing a book will find character and concept pages to continue that discussion.

We picked Flat Earth Machine for people who like philosophical speculation and competing interpretations. Approach its cosmology as fiction, with curiosity about how the world is constructed. A useful question for a reading group is whether witnessing something gives a character freedom, responsibility, or both.

THIS ONE IS FOR

Readers of metaphysical and philosophical science fiction who enjoy questions about memory, perception and constructed realities. Its cosmology is part of a speculative fictional world.

Selection note based on the author’s introduction and published Witness character material, rather than a full-book review.

MEET THE FEATURED AUTHOR

Gary J. Head

Gary J. Head is the author of Flat Earth Machine. His official biography describes an engineering career spanning automotive, mining, aviation and heavy industry, followed by an interest in philosophy, symbolism and consciousness. His website extends the novel through character and fictional-world material.
