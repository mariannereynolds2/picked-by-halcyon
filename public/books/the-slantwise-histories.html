The Slantwise Histories: Collected Stories

by Alix E. Harrow

A collection of ghosts, legends and alternate histories asks who gets remembered—and who is left outside the official story.

Preorder on Amazon (opens in a new tab)Read why we picked it

Forthcoming: October 20, 2026. Available to preorder; not yet released.

Why we picked it

The people history forgets still have stories to tell

History can look reassuringly orderly from a distance. There are names we are taught to remember, wars that receive chapters, and people whose lives become footnotes. Fantasy is uniquely able to put the forgotten back in the center of the story. In The Slantwise Histories, Alix E. Harrow lets magic unsettle our assumptions about what the past was and whose version deserves to survive.

The book brings together celebrated earlier short fiction and five new stories. Ghosts, dreamers, soldiers, mothers, workers and unlikely places move through the collection. It includes the Hugo-winning A Witch's Guide to Escape: A Practical Compendium of Portal Fantasies as well as other stories that approach myth, history and ordinary acts of resistance from unexpected angles.

A collection of short stories is especially useful for a book club that wants room to compare perspectives. Instead of asking everyone to finish an enormous fantasy epic, members can choose two stories and consider how each handles agency, power and the telling of legends. When a story changes the rules of history, is that an escape from reality or a way of revealing something real about it?

We picked this as a welcoming route into Harrow's imaginative range. Readers familiar with her novels may discover a more concentrated version of her themes, while newcomers can begin with one story at a time. The US collection is scheduled for October 20, 2026, so this is a clearly labeled preorder recommendation, not a claim to have read an unreleased volume.

Who it’s for

Fantasy clubs, folklore readers, short-story groups and readers interested in women, workers, memory and the relationship between myth and history. A collection, not a single-plot fantasy novel.

Book club conversation

When a tale gives the overlooked a voice, is it rewriting history or exposing how incomplete official history has always been?

About the author

Alix E. Harrow

Alix E. Harrow is a Hugo Award-winning fantasy writer and the author of The Ten Thousand Doors of January, The Once and Future Witches, Starling House and The Everlasting. Her stories often explore folklore, history and the ways marginalized people preserve hope and agency. The Slantwise Histories collects much of her short fiction, including award-winning work and five new stories, and accompanies a scheduled October 2026 author tour.
