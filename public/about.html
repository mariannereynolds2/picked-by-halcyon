Fewer recommendations. Better reasons.

Picked by Halcyon is the reader-facing desk of Halcyon Literary House. We pick a few books at a time and give each one a page that lasts.

A book feature should outlast a post.

Social posts disappear in a day. Here, every book keeps its own page, its own place on a shelf, and a spot in the archive. Readers can browse by mood or genre and find authors long after a launch week ends.

We write plainly about what a book is, who will enjoy it, and what to know before buying, including content notes and whether it is part of a series. We would rather you skip a book that is wrong for you than buy it on hype.

Authors never pay to be featured. We choose books because we think readers will want them. Our editorial standards explain how.

Our team only emails from addresses ending in @halcyonliterary.com. If you are unsure whether a message is from us, forward it to hello@halcyonliterary.com.
