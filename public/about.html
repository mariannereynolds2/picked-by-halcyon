ABOUT PICKED BY HALCYON

Built for readers who want fewer, better recommendations.

THE IDEA

A book feature should last longer than a post.

Social posts disappear quickly. Here, every book can keep its own page, genre shelf and place in the archive. Readers can browse by mood or genre, authors can be discovered later, and the publication stays useful even when no algorithm is helping.
