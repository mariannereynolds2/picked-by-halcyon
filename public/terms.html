Terms

The short version: features are free, you keep the rights to your work, and you can ask us to remove your feature at any time.

Features are free

Submitting a book and being featured on Picked by Halcyon are free. We do not charge for placement and do not sell picks.

No guarantee of selection

We pick books at our own editorial discretion. We reply to every submission within 7 days, but sending a book does not guarantee a feature.

What you allow us to do

When you submit, you confirm that you are the author or have the author’s permission, and that you have the right to share the text and images you send. You allow Picked by Halcyon to use them, free of charge and without exclusivity, to publish and promote your feature. You keep all rights to your work.

Changes and removal

We may edit submitted text for length and clarity. You can ask us to correct or remove your feature at any time by emailing hello@halcyonliterary.com.

Buying books

Buy links take you to the stores and author sites that sell each book. Prices, formats and availability are set by those sellers and may change.

Contact

Questions about these terms: hello@halcyonliterary.com.
