Questions authors ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no fee to submit and no fee to be featured, now or later. We do not sell placement on Picked by Halcyon.

Do you feature every book?

No. We pick books we think our readers will want, so we turn some down. We tell you either way, and you are welcome to send another book later.

Does my book need to be new?

No. Backlist books are welcome, as long as readers can still buy them.

I am self-published. Can I submit?

Yes. Most of the authors we feature are independent, and many are self-published.

My book is in French. Can I still send it?

Yes. Fill in the form in English or French and tell us the language of the book. Our features are written in English, so mention any English edition or translation.

What happens after my feature goes live?

We send you the link. The page stays up permanently and moves to the archive and your genre shelf when new picks arrive. Send corrections to hello@halcyonliterary.com at any time.

Not ready to submit?

Ask us anything about how features work, what we look for, or whether your book might fit.
