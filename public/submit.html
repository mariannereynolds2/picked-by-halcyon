What’s the catch?

There isn’t one, and we understand why you would ask. Authors receive a lot of emails offering “free” promotion that turns into a bill.

Picked by Halcyon is run by Halcyon Literary House, a book-marketing company for independent authors. Our optional paid services, such as author websites and promotion campaigns, pay for this site. A feature never depends on buying anything, and we do not sell placement on Picked by Halcyon.

We will never ask you for

  • Money to submit, to be featured, or to be “reviewed”
  • Card or bank details
  • Your book file (manuscript, ePub or PDF) by email
  • Passwords or logins to Amazon, KDP or any other account

Questions authors ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no fee to submit and no fee to be featured, now or later. We do not sell placement on Picked by Halcyon.

How do you choose books?

We pick books we think our readers will want: a clear premise, writing that holds up in the sample, and honest information we can pass on to readers. Our editorial standards explain it in full.

Do you feature every book?

No, so we tell you either way. If your book is not picked, you are welcome to send another one later.

Does my book need to be new?

No. Backlist books are welcome, as long as readers can still buy them.

I am self-published. Can I submit?

Yes. Most of the authors we feature are independent, and many are self-published.

My book is in French. Can I still send it?

Yes. Our features are written in English, so tell us about any English edition or translation. You can also use our French page.

How will I know if people read my feature?

We send you a private link that shows how many times your feature page has been viewed, over the last 30 days and in total.

Can I ask for changes or removal?

Yes. Email hello@halcyonliterary.com with corrections at any time, or ask us to take the feature down.

Not ready to submit?

Ask us anything about how features work, what we look for, or whether your book might fit.
