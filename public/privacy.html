Privacy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to have it removed. Written plainly.

Who we are

Picked by Halcyon (picked.halcyonliterary.com) is run by Halcyon Literary House. You can contact us about your data at hello@halcyonliterary.com.

What we collect

  • Newsletter: your email address, so we can send you new picks.
  • Book submissions: the details and images you send, so we can review your book and write a feature.
  • Messages: your name, email and message, so we can reply.
  • Page views: a count of how many times each page is viewed each day. This count does not include your name, email, IP address or any cookie.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The only cookie on this site is a sign-in cookie used by our editors.

Services we use

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Emails are sent through Resend. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your browser’s standard request information when a page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep newsletter emails until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you. We keep submissions and messages while they are useful for handling your book or question, and delete them on request.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to be removed from the newsletter, by emailing hello@halcyonliterary.com. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.
