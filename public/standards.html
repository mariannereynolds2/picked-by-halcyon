How we choose and write about books

The rules we follow for every pick, so readers and authors know exactly what a Picked by Halcyon feature means.

Why we pick a book

We pick books we think our readers will be glad to have found. We look for a clear premise, writing that holds up in the sample, real reader interest, and enough honest information to tell a reader what they are getting.

We never sell picks

No one can pay to be featured on Picked by Halcyon, and no one pays us to write a positive feature. Halcyon Literary House offers separate, optional services to authors. Buying one of them does not get a book picked, and declining them never counts against a book.

How we write a feature

  • We say why we picked the book in our own words, not by copying the blurb.
  • We say who the book suits, and who might prefer something else.
  • We include content notes and series information when a reader would want them before buying.
  • Some features are based on the book’s description, a sample, series information and published reader resources rather than a complete read. When that is the case, the feature says so.

Facts and sources

We only state facts we can check: publication details, series order, awards, book-club listings and so on. Where a feature draws on outside sources, we link to them.

Corrections and removal

If something in a feature is wrong, email hello@halcyonliterary.com and we will correct it. Authors can ask us to update or remove their feature at any time.

Images

We use cover images and author photos that authors send us, or that are published on the author’s or publisher’s official channels, and we credit the source where we can.
